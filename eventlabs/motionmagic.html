// ABOUT THE CAM

It is a robotic arm equipped with a mirrorless camera or GoPro (e.g., Sony, GoPro) that moves rapidly and precisely to film guests, often in high-speed, 240-frame-per-second slow motion — giving them a “red carpet” experience.

A minimum 10×10 ft area is required for safe operation and optimal movement of the robotic arm.

The robot is pre-programmed with various paths, allowing it to swoop in and out around a guest within seconds — capturing every angle in dramatic slow motion.

A full initialization and setup takes approximately 30 minutes. We recommend scheduling our arrival at least 45 minutes before your event begins.

A standard 3-prong, 110-volt, 60 Hz outlet is required. The floor surface must be solid, level, and secure for safe arm operation.

// RENTAL & SERVICE INFO

Rental services include the robot arm, a professional operator, lighting setup, and digital delivery of the videos. Props and additional lighting vary by package tier.

Footage is sent to guests instantly via SMS, Email, QR code, or AirDrop — so they can share to social media in seconds.
